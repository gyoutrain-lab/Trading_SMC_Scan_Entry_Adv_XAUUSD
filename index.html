Educational tool. Output is rule-based pattern detection, not financial advice; no strategy wins every trade and no filter can guarantee a setup is not a fake. "Verified" means every SMC gate (liquidity sweep, real structure shift, fresh order block, FVG, premium/discount, costs) passed and the replay edge is not negative. For gold and silver the Metals Desk adds session, volatility, chop, trend, news and margin-survival gates plus a direction lock; with a small balance even 0.01 lot can be too large for the stop, and the app will say so instead of showing an entry. Forex/metal feeds carry no true exchange volume: there, relative volume and profiles are range/time-at-price proxies and are labelled as such. Costs use typical spreads (override in Position Size). US holidays are not modelled. Forward-test on demo and manage risk. Data: Twelve Data REST API (forex, metals, stocks, ETF, crypto; futures/indices depend on your plan).
